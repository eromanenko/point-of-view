# Point of View Translate

## Description
This app is a helpful tool for players of the "Point of View" board game. It allows you to scan the QR codes on the physical game cards using your phone's camera, instantly showing you a translation of the text or question on the card in your preferred language.

You can easily switch between languages (English, German, Ukrainian, Russian) and the app will even change colors to match the card types (like the Spooky Festival expansion).

## Features
- **Quick Scanner**: Instantly read QR codes from your game cards.
- **Works Offline**: Once you install the app on your phone, you don't need an internet connection to use it during your game nights.
- **Multiple Languages**: Supports English, German, Ukrainian, and Russian.

## How to Set Up the App (Using GitHub Pages)

To easily share and install this app on your phone, you can host it for free using GitHub Pages. Here is a simple, step-by-step guide.

### 1. Fork this Repository
1. If you haven't already, create a free account on [GitHub](https://github.com/) and log in.
2. Go to the main page of this project on GitHub.
3. Click the **"Fork"** button in the top right corner of the page. This creates a personal copy of the app in your own GitHub account.
4. Click **"Create fork"** to confirm.

### 2. Turn on GitHub Pages
1. Go to your repository on GitHub.
2. Click on the **Settings** tab near the top right.
3. On the left-hand menu, scroll down and click on **Pages**.
4. Under the "Build and deployment" section, look for **Source** and select **Deploy from a branch**.
5. Under **Branch**, select `main` (or `master`) from the dropdown, leave the folder as `/ (root)`, and click **Save**.
6. Wait a minute or two. At the top of the Pages settings, you will eventually see a message that says: *"Your site is live at https://[your-username].github.io/point-of-view/"*.
7. Click the link to open the app in your browser.

### 3. Install the App on Your Phone
To get the best experience, you should install the app directly on your smartphone so it works offline and looks like a regular full-screen app.

1. Open your phone's web browser:
   - Use **Safari** if you are on an iPhone (iOS).
   - Use **Chrome** if you are on an Android phone.
2. Type in the live link you got from GitHub Pages (e.g., `https://[your-username].github.io/point-of-view/`).
3. **If you have an iPhone (Safari)**:
   - Tap the **Share** button at the bottom of the screen (it looks like a square with an arrow pointing up).
   - Scroll down the menu and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
   - Tap **"Add"** in the top right corner.
4. **If you have an Android (Chrome)**:
   - A message might pop up at the bottom asking if you want to "Add to Home screen". Just tap it.
   - If you don't see the message, tap the three dots in the top right corner of Chrome.
   - Tap **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install app"**, then confirm.
5. Close your browser and look at your phone's home screen. You will see the new app icon! Tap it to open the scanner. It will now work completely offline.
